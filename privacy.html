隱私政策

版本 1.0 · 2026-09-15

一句話

驗屋手帳沒有帳號、沒有自己的伺服器，也沒有任何分析、崩潰回報或廣告工具。你記的房屋資料、檢查結果、缺失紀錄與照片都留在自己的裝置上，不會被傳出去。

唯一會連上網路的是 Pro 買斷相關的兩件事：取得價格、確認你買過。詳見〈付費〉與〈網路權限〉兩節。

這個 App 沒有廣告，也不會有。

我們不做什麼

不要求註冊或登入，沒有帳號可以建立，也沒有地方讓你填 Email。

不申請定位、儲存空間、通訊錄、通話、簡訊或麥克風權限，不使用無障礙服務，也不讀取其他 App 的通知。

連相機權限都沒有申請：拍缺失照片是交給系統的相機 App 拍好再交回來，本 App 不會自己啟動相機。

沒有內建分析工具、沒有崩潰回報，也沒有第三方 SDK 在背景記錄你怎麼使用這個 App。

照片

照片來自系統相機或系統的相片選擇器，兩者都不需要儲存空間權限。照片寫進本 App 的私有目錄之前，會先移除 EXIF 裡的定位與其他中繼資料，之後只有這個 App 讀得到。

你在照片上畫的圈與箭頭另外存成線條資料，原始照片不會被改動。

檢查清單與規則資料

App 內建的檢查清單、保固年限與保留款比例，是隨安裝包提供的資料檔，並標示資料日期。核對結果全部是參考值，不會依你的資料向任何地方查詢，也不構成法律意見。

本 App 為屋主自助紀錄工具，不取代專業驗屋。保固、保留款以契約與內政部公告為準。

你的資料存在哪裡

房屋資料、檢查結果、缺失紀錄、照片與標記、複驗輪次、保固與保留款設定、提醒時間與所有偏好，都存在 App 自己的本機儲存空間裡，只有這個 App 讀得到。移除 App 就會一併刪除。

App 已關閉 Android 系統的「自動備份」與裝置轉移，所以系統不會把 App 的資料與照片複製到你的 Google 雲端硬碟或新手機。要保留一份副本，請用 App 內的「匯出備份」。

通知權限

用來在複驗前一天、建商承諾修繕日、保固到期前與保留款相關日期提醒你。

所有通知都是在你的裝置上排程與顯示的本機通知，不經過任何推播服務，也沒有任何內容離開裝置。

不給這個權限只會讓提醒不出現，其他功能照常。

精確鬧鐘與開機權限

App 使用 Android 的精確鬧鐘，在設定頁顯示的時間發出提醒；若你不允許精確鬧鐘，提醒會改在前後 15 分鐘的視窗內送出。開機完成的權限用來在手機重開後把提醒重新排好。兩者都只在裝置上運作。

網路權限

App 會在兩個時候連上網路：你打開驗屋手帳 Pro 的畫面時，向 Google Play 取得買斷價格；以及每次啟動時，向 Google Play 確認你是否買過 Pro。這兩件事都不會送出你記的任何資料或照片。

其他功能 —— 檢查清單、缺失與照片標記、複驗輪次、保固與保留款倒數、提醒、PDF 與 CSV 匯出、備份匯出與匯入、桌面小工具 —— 全部在裝置上完成。

備份、PDF 與 CSV

備份檔是在你按下「匯出備份」的當下，於裝置上即時產生的 zip 檔，裡面有你的全部資料與照片。存到哪裡由你在系統的檔案選擇器裡決定，我們不會上傳，也不知道你放在哪裡或分享給誰。

匯入時只讀取你選的那一個檔案，一樣不經過網路。

PDF 報告與 CSV 都在裝置上產生，只有在你選擇分享或存檔時才會離開本 App；分享給誰、存到哪裡，都由你在系統的分享面板裡決定。

桌面小工具

小工具顯示的進度、缺失數與下一個提醒全部來自裝置上的 App 資料，不會為了小工具連線。

兒童

這個 App 不是為兒童設計的，對象是準備交屋的屋主。我們不蒐集任何人的個人資料，兒童也不例外。

付費

驗屋手帳 Pro 是一次買斷，交易一律由 Google Play 處理。App 只會收到「你是不是 Pro」這一項結果，不會接觸你的付款資訊，也沒有使用其他第三方付費服務。

App 會把「是 Pro」的結果記在裝置上，離線時最多沿用 7 天，之後再向 Google Play 確認一次。

退款依 Google Play 的政策辦理。

政策的變更

政策若有修改，會更新上面的版本與日期，並隨 App 更新一起發布。

Privacy Policy

Version 1.0 · 2026-09-15

In short

Keyproof has no accounts, no server of our own, and no analytics, crash reporting or advertising of any kind. Your home details, checklist results, defects and photos stay on your own device and are never sent anywhere.

The only thing that goes online is the Pro unlock itself: fetching its price and checking whether you own it. See the Payments and Internet permission sections.

There are no ads in this app, and there will not be.

What we do not do

No sign-up, no login, no account to create, and nowhere to enter an email address.

No location, storage, contacts, phone, SMS or microphone permission, no accessibility service, and the app never reads other apps' notifications.

Not even the camera permission: defect photos are taken by the system camera app and handed back; this app never runs the camera itself.

No analytics, no crash reporting, and no third-party SDK recording how you use the app.

Photos

Photos come from the system camera or the system photo picker, neither of which needs a storage permission. Before a photo is written to the app's private folder, its EXIF location and other metadata are removed; after that only this app can read it.

Circles and arrows you draw on a photo are stored separately as line data; the original photo is never altered.

Checklist and rule data

The built-in checklist, warranty years and retention rate are a data file shipped with the app, labelled with its data date. Every result is a reference value; nothing about your data is looked up anywhere, and none of it is legal advice.

Keyproof is a self-help notebook for owners and does not replace a professional inspection. Warranty and retention figures follow your contract and the Ministry of the Interior announcements.

Where your data lives

Home details, checklist results, defects, photos and markup, re-inspection rounds, warranty and retention settings, reminder times and every preference are held in the app's own local storage, readable only by this app. Uninstalling the app deletes them.

The app opts out of Android Auto Backup and device transfer, so the system will not copy its data or photos to your Google Drive or a new phone. To keep a copy, use "Export backup" inside the app.

Notification permission

Used to remind you the day before a re-inspection, on the builder's promised repair date, before a warranty expires and on retention-related dates.

Every notification is scheduled and shown locally on your device. No push service is involved and nothing leaves the device.

Declining it only means reminders will not appear; everything else works as usual.

Exact alarm and boot permissions

The app uses Android's exact alarms to remind you at the times shown in Settings; if you decline exact alarms, reminders arrive within a 15-minute window instead. The boot-completed permission re-schedules reminders after a restart. Both work entirely on the device.

Internet permission

The app goes online in two situations: when you open the Keyproof Pro screen, to fetch the price from Google Play; and at each launch, to check with Google Play whether you own Pro. Neither sends any of your data or photos.

Everything else — the checklist, defects and photo markup, re-inspection rounds, warranty and retention countdowns, reminders, PDF and CSV export, backup export and import, the home-screen widget — happens on your device.

Backups, PDF and CSV

The backup is a zip file generated on your device the moment you tap "Export backup". It contains all your data and photos. Where it goes is decided by you in the system file picker; we never upload it and have no idea where you keep it or who you share it with.

Importing reads only the single file you pick, again without any network connection.

PDF reports and CSV files are generated on the device and leave the app only when you choose to share or save them; who receives them is decided by you in the system share sheet.

Home-screen widget

Everything the widget shows — progress, defect count, the next reminder — comes from the app's data on your device. It never connects to the network.

Children

This app is not designed for children; it is for owners preparing for handover. We collect no personal data from anyone, children included.

Payments

Keyproof Pro is a one-time purchase handled by Google Play. The app receives only whether you own Pro, never your payment details, and no other payment service is involved.

The app remembers the Pro result on the device and keeps using it for up to 7 days while offline, then checks with Google Play again.

Refunds follow Google Play's policy.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the version and date above are updated and shipped with an app update.
